## Reproduced defects and changes

- SABA accepted a stable visible DOM viewport as a complete replacement, losing socket-only inventory. Fresh DOM also deferred recovery of a missing native field table. Production now requires independently validated Today/Early collector completion for full catalog authority. Socket/DOM updates retain the collector and original acquisition timestamps; an empty socket partition cannot clear the prematch collector.
- SBOBET published the initial Live+Today roster before the validated All Dates roster arrived. Production now holds initial publication until Early completion, including an explicitly validated empty Early result. This preserves a retained complete catalog while the replacement collects.
- Exact league labels and team variants split valid fixtures. Added specific competition aliases and Lausanne Sports/Lausanne-Sport, Rodez AF/Rodez Aveyron aliases. Women, reserves, age groups, regulation segments, market families, lines and settlement checks remain required.
- The actual SABA Sports document at `c0z0oa.bpd3a3fn.com` exposed a public clock `04:24:02AM Sep 09, 2026 GMT +7`, read through Windows UI Automation without navigating or making provider requests. The clock is in the nested SL88SW document, with parent `#systemTime.c-header__time`. DOM normalization hardcoded GMT+8. Boca Juniors–Sao Paulo and Criciuma–Juventude were exactly one hour early against BTI/APSPORT/SBOBET. Explicit public timezone metadata now survives visible DOM and collector schemas, controls UTC conversion and zoned date boundaries, and participates in owner identity checks. Missing/conflicting timezone rejects a new collector before changing retained coverage or quote clocks. Legacy records with no field keep the former offset; new records always supply an offset or explicit null. No match-window widening is used.
- Main-thread ranking repeated full scans of the global movement list for every row. A weakly cached event/row index now avoids those scans. The ranking clock commits with worker/preflight results, and unchanged verified maps do not cause another ranking pass. Preflight skips row enumeration when fewer than two eligible providers exist. The same-process synthetic benchmark of actual ranking code, 650 events × 20 rows and 13,000 movements, measured warm passes at 1,882 → 1,198 ms with identical ticket plans, states, ordering and movement magnitudes. This is a component benchmark, not a browser frame-time claim.
- A finished collector does not rerun after socket-only closure in the same epoch. A valid retained complete collector now allows native reset/done to restore baseline authority without another sweep; its acquisition and retention clocks stay unchanged. A new source epoch still needs its own collector proof.
- The extension build previously extracted only the capture template and lost its shared timezone binding. The build now bundles/imports the capture module before inlining the fully resolved expression. The resulting bundle has the public clock selector and no unresolved capture placeholder.
- Live verification exposed slow collector completion: already captured owners with no eligible expansion control consumed the four-owner action budget and caused repeated Today/Early restoration. Production now completes up to 512 such owners in memory per slice, checking the existing five-second deadline, while actual expansion actions remain capped at Today 1 / Early 4. The 650-owner regression finishes in two slices with four roster reads, no expansion calls, and identical terminal membership and quote clocks. Mixed owners still respect the action cap. Both collector/observer suites were independently rerun: 99 passing tests.
